## What it does

`main.py` writes two lines to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically, with no network access:

1. Line 1 explains a traffic jam to a 10-year-old in a single vivid sentence, built on one analogy: a herd of metal elephants standing trunk to tail on the road.
2. Line 2 says in one sentence why that analogy works — what it maps (cars packed close together, creeping forward) and what it leaves out (the drivers, the engines, and the reason the road jammed).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Two runs produce byte-identical output. The entry is 293 characters, within the 1–400 character gate.
